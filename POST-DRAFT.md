A tree with a marked edge is determined by its deletion forest and its contraction with the merged vertex marked. This rules out a construction that searches for counterexamples to Stanley’s tree conjecture by exchanging attachment roots. [paper link]

## Follow-up thread

1. Two specified edges in a tree can be recovered from three weighted contraction records. The three-edge version also holds when the specified edges form a connected subtree. These results rule out matching the expansion terms one by one. [paper link]
2. We checked 313,000 pairs of three-copy joins with matching double contractions. Every pair differed in its component-size counts using at most four cuts.
3. A smallest counterexample built by sliding a fixed graft across two edges would need at least 40 vertices. The bound uses two endpoint invariants and an exhaustive check of small rooted trees. Stanley’s conjecture remains open. [replay link]
